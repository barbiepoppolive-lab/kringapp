About Kring

Kring (กริ๊ง!) is an online marketplace for booking short activities with ID-checked locals and licensed tour companies in Bangkok and Pattaya, always in public places. Kring, let's go!

What we do

Travellers pick an activity, such as a street-food walk, a market visit, a cooking class or a Muay Thai lesson. There is one price per activity for 1–2 people. You book in the app, meet at a public meeting point, touch phones to check in (กริ๊ง!) and the activity starts.

How payment works

Online, you pay only Kring's booking fee, through our payment providers. You pay the activity price straight to your host by PromptPay QR at the meeting point, after check-in. For tours run by licensed tour companies, the tour price is paid at the company's counter.

What Kring is not

Kring is for activities only. It is not a dating or companionship app. There is no booking by the hour, and no meeting at hotels, rooms or homes.

Launch

The app opens in November 2026 in Bangkok and Pattaya.

Who runs Kring

Kring is run by KRING, a sole proprietorship registered as an MSME in India, under NIC 79900 (travel agency, tour operator and other reservation service activities).
