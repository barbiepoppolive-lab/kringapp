WHAT WE FOUND IN `luckyPoolRng.ts`⚠ 3 Hidden Flaws Today
Flaw 1: 86.68% of Spins Return 0 Coins
Current table: `2x (7.5%)`, `5x (4.0%)`, `10x (1.5%)`, `50x (0.2%)`, `100x (0.1%)`, `500x (0.02%)`. Total hit rate = 13.32%. Nearly 9 out of 10 taps give nothing!
