Make new friends in Ari, Bangkok

Ari is leafy, low-rise and slow: quiet sois, small cafés and people who actually live in the neighbourhood.

Open Kring →Free · LINE or email · 18+

What Ari is like

Ari sits on Phahonyothin Road, a few stops north of the city centre. The sois behind the BTS station are lined with old trees, houses turned into cafés, small galleries and design shops. It is one of the easiest parts of Bangkok to walk, and Chatuchak Weekend Market is two BTS stops away.

Places people love here

  • Soi Ari and Soi Ari Samphan
  • House-turned-cafés under the big trees
  • Small galleries and design shops
  • Chatuchak Weekend Market, two stops north

Getting there

BTS Ari (Sukhumvit line).

How Kring works in Ari

  1. Make your card: your name, your neighbourhoods, what you are into.
  2. Post a short Reel and tag the real place.
  3. Talk to people who love the same places. Chats translate between Thai and English.

Good to know

Is Kring free?
Yes. Making a card, posting Reels and chatting are free.
Do I need to speak Thai?
No. Chats translate between Thai and English, so locals and travellers can talk.
Where do people get together?
Always in public places such as cafés, markets and parks. Never private addresses.
When does Kring open?
Kring opens in Bangkok on 1 December 2026. You can make your card now.
