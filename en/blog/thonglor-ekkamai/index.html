Thonglor and Ekkamai: Where to Hang Out and Make Friends

Thonglor and Ekkamai are among the liveliest areas on Sukhumvit, good for hanging out with friends at weekends and after work. The easiest starting point is theCOMMONS on Thong Lo Soi 17, open 8am to 1am, before exploring the side sois around it.

By the Kring team · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

✓ Facts on this page last checked 17 Nov 2026 · we re-check this page every 3 months

Thonglor vs Ekkamai

Thonglor is Sukhumvit Soi 55 and Ekkamai is Sukhumvit Soi 63. They run parallel, with side sois linking them in several places; use BTS Thong Lo or BTS Ekkamai. Thonglor is the more polished of the two, with lots of design-led places, while Ekkamai is a little more relaxed and home to the Eastern Bus Terminal. See our Thonglor area page for an overview.

theCOMMONS: the easy starting point

theCOMMONS is at 335 Thong Lo Soi 17, open 8am–1am, with each vendor setting its own hours. It is a multi-level, open-air community mall with coffee, food and shared seating: ideal for getting a few new friends together, because it is busy, bright and has seats all day.

From BTS Thong Lo Exit 3 it is a 5–10 minute taxi or ride-hailing trip, or a 15–20 minute walk.

Morning runs in Thonglor

Meep Meep Run Club holds twice-monthly morning sessions with theCOMMONS (some are at other branches, so check the club's Instagram), starting around 7am; arrive 15 minutes early for the briefing, and note that some city routes need pre-booking (per Time Out, February 2026). Afterwards, coffee is in the same building. It is a great way to get to know people who live nearby.

Working in Thonglor and Ekkamai

UnionSPACE WorkPod has branches in both Ekkamai and Thong Lo, from 250 baht per 24 hours (September 2026). Many remote workers live here because there is so much to do after work. More in Bangkok for digital nomads.

Exploring the area well

  • Soi Thong Lo is long and sunny. At midday, take a motorbike taxi from the soi entrance or a ride-hailing car, then walk the side sois.
  • Evenings are when the area comes alive: lights on, people sitting out front.
  • Watch for design markets and pop-ups at the community malls; they are usually announced on Instagram.
  • In a group, choose places with long tables or outdoor seating; it is easier to talk than in small rooms with loud music.

Making friends here

  • Join a morning run club instead of trying to talk to strangers in busy places late at night.
  • Use the same co-working space regularly; the area is full of remote workers.
  • Bring a few new friends together at theCOMMONS in the daytime: bright, busy and easy to talk.
  • Suggest a walk in Benjakitti or Benchasiri Park nearby. See Sukhumvit and Benjakitti.

If you stay out late

Thonglor and Ekkamai are busiest at night. If you stay late with people you have just met:

  • Stick to busy, well-lit places, and do not follow anyone on to somewhere you do not know.
  • Keep your own drink with you; do not leave it and come back to it.
  • Get home with a ride-hailing app that shows the driver and route, and share your location with a friend.

An easy Sunday here

  • 08:00 morning coffee at theCOMMONS before the crowds.
  • 10:00 wander the side sois between Thonglor and Ekkamai for design shops and handsome houses.
  • 12:00 escape the heat somewhere air-conditioned, or a co-working space if work calls.
  • 17:00 one BTS stop to Phrom Phong for a walk in Benchasiri Park as the sun softens.

If it rains

theCOMMONS is covered and multi-level, so you can sit out a downpour there, or switch to board games or a co-working space nearby. Do not call a car the moment rain starts: that is exactly when rides are hardest to find and traffic is worst.

A note on timing

Traffic here is heavy on Friday evenings. For after-work plans, choose somewhere near the BTS or allow an extra half hour.

FAQ

What are theCOMMONS Thonglor opening hours?

According to theCOMMONS, 8am to 1am, with each vendor setting its own hours.

Which BTS station is Thonglor?

BTS Thong Lo or BTS Ekkamai on the Sukhumvit line. theCOMMONS is on Thong Lo Soi 17, about 5–10 minutes by car from BTS Thong Lo Exit 3.

What is the difference between Thonglor and Ekkamai?

Thonglor (Sukhumvit 55) is more polished and design-led; Ekkamai (Sukhumvit 63) is more relaxed. Side sois connect them.

Is there a run club in Thonglor?

Meep Meep Run Club runs twice a month with theCOMMONS, per Time Out (February 2026); check its Instagram for which branch.
