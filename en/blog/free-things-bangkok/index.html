Free Things to Do in Bangkok: 12 Places With No Entry Fee

Bangkok has more great free places than most people expect: the Bangkok Art and Culture Centre, Chao Phraya Sky Park, Lumphini and Benjakitti parks and the street art of Talat Noi, among others. Pay only for transport and food, and you can spend a whole day out with friends.

By the Kring team · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

✓ Hours, prices and transport on this page last checked 17 Nov 2026 · we re-check this page every month

Why this list matters

Going out with friends often gets expensive fast. Everything here has no entry fee according to the latest information we checked, so you can see friends more often without worrying about money. Transport and food are extra.

Art and ideas

1. Bangkok Art and Culture Centre (BACC) at Siam: free exhibitions except some special events, open Tuesday to Sunday 10:00–20:00, linked to BTS National Stadium. 2. The TCDC rooftop garden at the Grand Postal Building, Bang Rak: Time Out lists the rooftop as free to enter (the library inside is paid). Closed Mondays. 3. Small galleries along Charoen Krung and in Ari: many are free and change shows often.

Parks and skywalks

4. Lumphini Park: open 04:30–21:00, with a loop of about 2.5 km. 5. Benjakitti Forest Park: 72 hectares with elevated walkways over the wetlands. 6. The Green Bridge: a 1.3 km skywalk linking Lumphini and Benjakitti. 7. Benchasiri Park by BTS Phrom Phong: open 05:00–21:00, with twelve contemporary sculptures. 8. Chao Phraya Sky Park on the Phra Pok Klao Bridge: free, open daily 05:00–20:00 per the latest listing, with a walk across to the Thonburi side.

Old streets and street art

9. Talat Noi: the street-art walls of Soi Chao Sua Son and old riverside lanes. Free to wander; just respect the homes people live in. 10. Chatuchak Weekend Market: free to browse, Saturday and Sunday 9:00–18:00. See Chatuchak with friends.

Free activities

11. Run clubs: many are free with no sign-up, such as SarDine Run Club on Saturdays. See running in Bangkok. 12. Language exchanges: some groups hold free Thai–English exchanges in the park, for practice and new friends at once.

A low-budget Saturday with friends

  • 07:00 run with a club (free).
  • 10:30 BACC at Siam (free).
  • 14:00 MRT to Hua Lamphong and a walk around Talat Noi (free).
  • 17:00 Chao Phraya Sky Park for sunset (free).

All day, you pay only for the train and two meals.

A free Sunday on the Sukhumvit side

  • 07:00 walk Benchasiri Park by BTS Phrom Phong.
  • 09:00 BTS to Asok, into Benjakitti and up onto the skywalks.
  • 10:30 follow the Green Bridge on to Lumphini Park.
  • 12:00 lunch around Sala Daeng, then home before the midday heat.

Free, so respect it

Many free places stay that way because everyone looks after them. Take your rubbish with you, do not climb on park sculptures, keep quiet in residential lanes and do not feed animals in the parks, such as Lumphini's monitor lizards. That keeps the free spots free, and lovely, for a long time.

More ways to save

  • Take the BTS, MRT and river boats instead of taxis: faster and cheaper.
  • Carry a water bottle and refill it where you can.
  • Go on weekdays if you can: fewer people and no queues.
  • Share a ride if you are heading somewhere far from the rail lines.
  • Follow BACC and other creative spaces online: many exhibition openings and talks are free, and they are easy places to start chatting with new people.
  • If a friendly stranger says the place you are heading to is closed today and offers to take you somewhere else, decline politely and check for yourself.

FAQ

What are the best free things to do in Bangkok?

The Bangkok Art and Culture Centre, Chao Phraya Sky Park, Lumphini, Benjakitti and Benchasiri parks, the TCDC rooftop garden and Talat Noi's street art.

Is the Bangkok Art and Culture Centre free?

Exhibitions are free except some special events. It opens Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–20:00.

Is Chao Phraya Sky Park free?

Yes, entry is free. The latest listing says it is open daily from 05:00 to 20:00.

Are there free activities for making friends in Bangkok?

Yes. Many run clubs are free, and some language-exchange groups charge nothing.
