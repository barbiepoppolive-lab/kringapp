Bangkok Night Markets 2027: Where to Go With Friends

The most fun night markets for a group in Bangkok right now are Jodd Fairs Ratchada, open daily from about 17:00 to 01:00 near MRT Thailand Cultural Centre; the Srinakarin Train Night Market, Thursday to Sunday; and riverside Asiatique, with its Ferris wheel and free shuttle boat.

By the Kring team · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

Why night markets are made for groups

Night markets are where Bangkok unwinds after work: the air cools, there is music, things to browse and long shared tables. Three or four people is perfect: split up to explore, then regroup at a table. It is also a great plan with a new group of friends, because there is always something to look at and talk about.

Jodd Fairs Ratchada

  • Open: daily, roughly 17:00 to 01:00.
  • Getting there: MRT Thailand Cultural Centre, Exit 4, then about a three-minute walk; it sits beside Big C Place Ratchadaphisek.
  • Vibe: neat rows of stalls, bright lights and plenty of shared seating, with a mix of Thais and visitors.
  • The old Jodd Fairs Rama 9 site closed in January 2025 when its lease ended, so do not head there.

Srinakarin Train Night Market (Talad Rot Fai Srinakarin)

  • Open: Thursday to Sunday, roughly 17:00 to 01:00; closed Monday to Wednesday. Many stalls open late, so it is livelier after 7pm.
  • Where: Srinakarin Soi 51, behind Seacon Square.
  • Getting there: the MRT Yellow Line to Suan Luang Rama IX station, which is close to the market, or a ride-hailing car. Show the driver the Thai name: ตลาดนัดรถไฟ ศรีนครินทร์.
  • Vibe: vintage, collectables, classic cars and second-hand fashion, great for treasure hunters.

Asiatique The Riverfront

  • Open: daily, 16:00 to midnight.
  • Getting there: the free shuttle boat from Sathorn Pier, about 10–15 minutes along the Chao Phraya.
  • Highlight: the 60-metre Asiatique Sky Ferris Wheel; a ride lasts about 15–20 minutes, with 360-degree night views.
  • Best for: a market, the river and a view in one evening, with wide, easy walkways.

Quick comparison

  • Closest to rail: Jodd Fairs Ratchada.
  • Vintage and collectables: the Srinakarin Train Night Market.
  • River views and a Ferris wheel: Asiatique.
  • Daytime at weekends: Chatuchak. See Chatuchak with friends.

Night markets with friends, done right

  • Agree on a regroup point before splitting up.
  • Set a similar budget so nobody feels awkward.
  • Carry small cash; many stalls take PromptPay, but some are cash-only.
  • Share locations in your LINE group while you are apart.
  • Head home before the last train, or book a ride through an app.

Staying safe at night

  • Keep your phone and cards in front of you; crowded markets attract opportunists.
  • Agree on a time to leave, and either leave together or message when you are home.
  • Booking through an app shows the driver's name and route, safer than flagging a car late at night.
  • If you have only just met online, make your own way there and back.

Match the market to your group

  • People who have just become friends: Jodd Fairs Ratchada, easy to reach, lots of tables, easy to get home.
  • Vintage and fashion lovers: the Srinakarin Train Night Market. It takes longer to reach, but it is worth it.
  • The photo crowd: Asiatique, for the river, the Ferris wheel and the boat ride.
  • Mixed Thai and foreign groups: Jodd Fairs or Asiatique, with plenty of English signage and vendors used to visitors.

A riverside alternative

To start with a sunset, stop at Chao Phraya Sky Park first, then take a boat down to Sathorn. See sunset spots in Bangkok. Or wander Yaowarat as the neon comes on.

FAQ

Where is Jodd Fairs now, and when does it open?

Jodd Fairs Ratchada opens daily from about 17:00 to 01:00, near MRT Thailand Cultural Centre Exit 4. The Rama 9 site closed in January 2025.

When is the Srinakarin Train Night Market open?

Thursday to Sunday, roughly 17:00 to 01:00; closed Monday to Wednesday.

How do I get to Asiatique?

Take the free shuttle boat from Sathorn Pier, about 10–15 minutes. Asiatique opens daily from 16:00 to midnight.

Which night market is closest to a train station?

Jodd Fairs Ratchada, about three minutes on foot from MRT Thailand Cultural Centre Exit 4.
