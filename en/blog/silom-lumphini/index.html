Silom and Lumphini Park 2027: Hanging Out in Central Bangkok

Silom and Lumphini Park are the easiest central spot to hang out with friends: BTS Sala Daeng and MRT Si Lom sit side by side, and the park is open daily 04:30–21:00 with a running loop, pedal boats and the Green Bridge to Benjakitti. After work, a walk in the park beats another mall.

By the Kring team · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

Why Silom works for hanging out

Silom is an office district, easy to reach from anywhere. BTS Sala Daeng and MRT Si Lom bring you to the park's corner, and MRT Lumphini serves the far side at the Witthayu intersection. It is a natural halfway point for friends who work in different parts of town.

Lumphini Park: more than a running track

  • The loop path: about 2.5 km, busy with runners morning and evening.
  • Pedal boats on the lake: a light, fun first plan with new friends.
  • Monitor lizards: hundreds live here, lounging by the water. Do not panic, but do not approach or feed them.
  • Bikes: allowed only 10:00–15:00 under park rules.
  • Hours: daily 04:30–21:00.

Walk the Green Bridge to Benjakitti

The Green Bridge is a 1.3 km elevated walking and cycling path from Sarasin junction (where Witthayu Road crosses Sarasin Road) to Benjakitti Forest Park. You walk above rooftops and treetops with towers all around, and the light is beautiful in the evening. It is perfect for long, easy conversations. More in Sukhumvit and Benjakitti.

Regular things happening in the park

  • Run clubs: several use Lumphini, including SarDine Run Club on some Saturdays (Time Out, February 2026). See running in Bangkok.
  • Language exchange: one group has held free Thai–English exchanges in the park on Sunday afternoons, gathering at MRT Si Lom Exit 1. Check its latest listing first.
  • Group exercise: in the evenings you will see aerobics and tai chi groups. Watch, or ask to join in.

After work in Silom

  • Walk an evening loop, then find food around Sala Daeng.
  • With a group of new friends, the Sala Daeng park gate is the easiest place to gather.

If it rains

Rain at Lumphini tends to be heavy but short, and there are several pavilions to shelter under. If it settles in, move to a café near Sala Daeng or take the train to somewhere indoors, like BACC at Siam.

A sample weekday evening

  • 18:00 gather at the Sala Daeng gate.
  • 18:15 walk one loop of the park, chatting as you go.
  • 19:00 go up onto the Green Bridge for the city lights, walk halfway and back.
  • 20:00 eat near the station, then head home by train.

Sunday morning in Lumphini

To see the park the way Bangkok people love it, come before 7am on a Sunday: runners, tai chi groups, dance classes and families out for a walk. The air is still cool and the light through the trees is gorgeous. Even just sitting on a bench by the lake and watching city life go by is a pleasure.

Safety

The park is busy in the morning and early evening but thins out near closing. Stick to well-lit paths and leave before it closes. With people you have only just met, choose daylight or early evening when plenty of people are around.

FAQ

What time does Lumphini Park open?

Daily from 04:30 to 21:00.

Which station is closest to Lumphini Park?

BTS Sala Daeng or MRT Si Lom on the Rama IV side, and MRT Lumphini on the Witthayu side.

How long is the Green Bridge?

About 1.3 km, from Lumphini Park at Sarasin junction to Benjakitti Forest Park, for walking and cycling.

Can I cycle in Lumphini Park?

Only between 10:00 and 15:00, under park rules.
