Sukhumvit, Asok to Phrom Phong: Parks and Places to Hang Out

Hanging out around Asok or Phrom Phong does not have to end in a mall. The 72-hectare Benjakitti Forest Park is near BTS Asok and MRT Queen Sirikit National Convention Centre, and Benchasiri Park sits right by BTS Phrom Phong, open 05:00–21:00 and free.

By the Kring team · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

More than malls

Sukhumvit between Asok and Phrom Phong is home to huge numbers of office workers and foreign residents. Terminal 21 at Asok and the Emporium and EmQuartier at Phrom Phong are landmarks everyone knows, but for real conversation, the area's two parks are far better.

Getting there

  • BTS Asok / MRT Sukhumvit: walk to Benjakitti's Ratchadaphisek Road side.
  • MRT Queen Sirikit National Convention Centre: close to Benjakitti's Rama IV side.
  • BTS Phrom Phong: the street-level exit onto Sukhumvit Road brings you straight to Benchasiri Park.

Benjakitti Forest Park

  • 72 hectares on Ratchadaphisek Road between Rama IV and Sukhumvit, fully opened in 2022.
  • Close to BTS Asok, MRT Sukhumvit and MRT Queen Sirikit National Convention Centre.
  • Elevated walkways over the wetlands make you feel outside the city, with towers all around.
  • The Green Bridge, 1.3 km long, links it to Lumphini Park for walking or cycling. See Silom and Lumphini Park.

Benchasiri Park

Right beside the Emporium, at the street-level exit of BTS Phrom Phong. Open 05:00–21:00 and free, with twelve contemporary sculptures by Thai artists, a small lake, a central lawn, jogging paths and outdoor fitness equipment. It is compact, walkable end to end in under an hour, and perfect for a stroll after lunch or before the mall.

Morning runs with the neighbourhood

Ugly Running Training Club (URT) runs most mornings around 6:30am at Gate 3 of Benjakitti Park by the stretching zone, and SarDine Run Club comes to Benjakitti on some Saturdays (Time Out, February 2026). If you live nearby, this is the fastest way to make local friends. More in running in Bangkok.

Working nearby

UnionSPACE WorkPod has a Phrom Phong branch from 250 baht per 24 hours (September 2026). Finish work, then walk to the park.

If it rains

This is one of the easiest areas to sit out the rain: covered walkways link BTS stations with several malls, so you can keep moving without getting soaked. Right after the rain, Benjakitti is at its greenest and coolest, a perfect time to walk.

Hanging out beyond the mall

  • Saturday morning: run or walk at Benjakitti, then eat around Asok.
  • Afternoon: hide from the heat indoors during the hottest hours.
  • Evening: stroll Benchasiri Park, or walk Benjakitti's skywalk for the city lights.
  • Weekdays: after-work plans at Benchasiri, easy to find and right by the station.

Why parks beat malls with new friends

In a mall, people drift towards shop windows and conversation stalls. In a park, you walk at the same pace, there is no music to compete with and something to talk about at every turn: the views, the birds, the people working out. Hanging out with new friends feels easier, and it costs nothing.

Tips

  • Benjakitti is big; agree on exactly which gate to gather at.
  • Bring water; some walkways have no shade.
  • Paths get slippery after rain, so slow down.
  • To continue to Thonglor or Ekkamai, it is just one or two BTS stops. See Thonglor and Ekkamai.
  • Benjakitti is big and some stretches are quiet in the evening; keep to the main lit paths and leave by the gate nearest the train.

FAQ

Which station is closest to Benjakitti Park?

BTS Asok, MRT Sukhumvit and MRT Queen Sirikit National Convention Centre are all close.

What are Benchasiri Park's opening hours?

Daily from 05:00 to 21:00, free entry, right by BTS Phrom Phong.

Can I walk from Benjakitti to Lumphini Park?

Yes, via the elevated Green Bridge, about 1.3 km, for walking and cycling.

Is there a run club near Asok?

Yes. URT runs most mornings around 6:30am at Gate 3 of Benjakitti Park, per Time Out (February 2026).
