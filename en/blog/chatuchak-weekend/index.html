Chatuchak with Friends 2027: The Market, the Parks and a Saturday Plan

Chatuchak Weekend Market is fully open on Saturdays and Sundays from 9:00 to 18:00, reached via MRT Kamphaeng Phet, MRT Chatuchak Park or BTS Mo Chit. The best plan with friends is the market in the morning, a break from the midday heat, then the neighbouring parks in the late afternoon.

By the Kring team · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

How big is Chatuchak?

Around 15,000 stalls across 27 sections. You could walk all day and not see it all, which is why it is more fun with friends: you hunt together, decide together and share the carrying.

Opening days and hours

  • Saturday and Sunday, 9:00–18:00: the whole market.
  • Wednesday and Thursday, 7:00–18:00: the plant section only.
  • Friday night: wholesale, with restricted access.

These hours come from the market's own website. If you are going on any day but Saturday or Sunday, double-check before you leave.

Getting there

  • MRT Kamphaeng Phet (Exit 2): comes out right inside the market's western end.
  • MRT Chatuchak Park: the eastern end.
  • BTS Mo Chit: also near the eastern end.

With a group, agree on an exact gathering point, such as a specific station exit, because it is very crowded inside and hard to find each other.

How not to get lost

  • Photograph the section map at the entrance and note the section numbers you want.
  • Agree on a regroup point in case you get separated, such as the clock tower in the middle.
  • Follow the main walkways first, then turn into the small lanes in the sections you care about.
  • Split up by section and send photos to your LINE group; it is fun and saves time.

Best time to go

Arrive as it opens, before the heat and the crowds. Noon to 3pm is very hot and the lanes get little breeze; rest somewhere with a fan or air-con, drink plenty of water and carry on in the late afternoon.

The parks next door

The market sits on the south side of Chatuchak Park, close to Wachirabenchathat Park (Suan Rot Fai) and Queen Sirikit Park.

  • Suan Rot Fai: bike rental, shady cycle paths, a butterfly garden and open lawns for a picnic.
  • Chatuchak Park: lakeside paths to rest your legs after the market.
  • On some Saturdays, SarDine Run Club runs at Suan Rot Fai (Time Out, February 2026); finish and walk straight into the market. See running in Bangkok.

A first visit with new friends

Chatuchak works well for a new group because there is something to talk about at every step: odd finds, cute finds and stalls you each love. It is very crowded, though, so agree up front how long you will stay and pick two or three sections you are both into. To finish, head to Chatuchak Park by the lake, where you can actually hear each other.

A Saturday plan with friends

  • 07:00 run or cycle at Suan Rot Fai.
  • 09:00 into the market as it opens; do your must-see sections first.
  • 12:00 rest in the shade and compare finds.
  • 15:30 a second round as the sun softens.
  • 17:00 two BTS stops to Ari for a café to finish.

On a weekday

On Wednesdays and Thursdays only the plant section opens, from 7am. It is quiet and easy to walk, great if you are decorating a home. Chatuchak Park and Suan Rot Fai are open every day, and on weekday evenings locals come out to run and cycle, a completely different mood from the weekend.

Tips

  • Bring cash and a charged phone; many stalls also take Thai PromptPay transfers.
  • Wear comfortable shoes and breathable clothes.
  • Polite bargaining is fine at some stalls, but do not push too hard.
  • For Bangkok's after-dark markets, see night markets to go to with friends.

FAQ

What are Chatuchak Weekend Market's opening hours?

The whole market opens Saturday and Sunday, 9:00–18:00. On Wednesday and Thursday only the plant section opens, 7:00–18:00.

Which station is best for Chatuchak?

MRT Kamphaeng Phet for the western end, or MRT Chatuchak Park and BTS Mo Chit for the eastern end.

When is the best time to visit Chatuchak?

Right when it opens in the morning, when it is cooler and less crowded.

Are there parks near Chatuchak?

Yes. Chatuchak Park is right beside the market, and Suan Rot Fai, with bike rental, is close by.
