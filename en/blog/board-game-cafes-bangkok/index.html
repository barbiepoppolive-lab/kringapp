Board Game Cafés in Bangkok: Where to Play (Even Solo)

Bangkok's most convenient board-game cafés cluster around Siam, MBK and Victory Monument, including The Stronghold, Quest and Turn The Tables. Going alone is fine: staff at many cafés will teach you games, and a game is one of the easiest ways to talk to people you have just met.

By the Kring team · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

Why board games make friends

The hardest part of sitting with strangers is finding something to talk about. A board game fixes that instantly: everyone has something to say, something to laugh at, and nobody has to tell their life story first. Plenty of friendships start with losing the same game together.

Cafés worth a visit

From The Thaiger's October 2025 round-up. Check opening hours and current prices on each café's page before you go.

  • The Stronghold Siam, Siam Square Soi 9, walkable from BTS Siam. Good for after work or class.
  • Quest Board Game Café, 7th floor of MBK Center, about 60 baht per person per hour, near BTS National Stadium.
  • Turn The Tables Board Game Café, Soi Ratchawithi 3, near BTS Victory Monument.
  • More Than a Game Café, Rimtara Building, Rama 3 Road.

The first two are close together around Siam, the easiest area for a first visit. Board-game cafés open and close fairly often in Bangkok, so treat any list, including this one, as a starting point and confirm on the café's own page that it is still running before you travel across town.

Games that work with new people

  • Team word games such as Codenames: lots of players, constant talking within teams.
  • Bluffing games such as Coup or The Resistance: brilliant with five or more and guaranteed laughs.
  • Co-operative games, where everyone wins or loses together: great for people who dislike serious competition.
  • Quick-rules games you can explain in five minutes: ideal for tables with beginners.

Skip three-hour strategy epics the first time; there is less chatting and some people will flag.

Can you go alone?

Yes. What works:

  • Tell staff you are solo and happy to join another table; some cafés will help.
  • Go on Friday evenings or weekend afternoons, when it is busiest.
  • Ask staff to seat you at a table that is open to newcomers, rather than walking up to a group cold.
  • Follow cafés' pages; some run game nights specifically for people coming alone.

Going as a group

If you have new friends from an app or run club, a board-game café makes a great second hangout: indoors, air-conditioned, inexpensive and always something to do. Three to five people is the sweet spot.

Teach a game in five minutes

  • Start with the goal: how you win.
  • Explain only what a player can do on their turn.
  • Play one open-handed practice round that does not count.
  • Save the small rules for when they come up.

People who teach games well become the person every table wants, which is a great way to get to know newcomers.

Café etiquette

  • Put every piece back before returning a game.
  • Order drinks or snacks according to the café's rules.
  • Keep drinks away from cards and boards.
  • Some noise is fine; just do not drown out other tables.

After the game

From Siam, walk on to the Bangkok Art and Culture Centre, open until 20:00, or stay indoors all day when it pours. See rainy-season plans in Bangkok.

FAQ

How much do board-game cafés in Bangkok cost?

It varies by café. Quest at MBK charges about 60 baht per person per hour, per The Thaiger (October 2025). Check current prices with the café.

Can I go to a board-game café alone?

Yes. Tell staff you are solo; some cafés will find you a table or teach you a game. Friday evenings and weekends are busiest.

Which board-game cafés are near BTS Siam?

The Stronghold in Siam Square Soi 9, and Quest on the 7th floor of MBK Center near BTS National Stadium.

What games are best with people I have just met?

Team word games like Codenames, bluffing games like Coup and quick co-operative games.
