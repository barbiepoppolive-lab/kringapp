Rainy Season in Bangkok 2027: Plans That Still Work

Bangkok's rainy season usually starts around mid-May and ends around mid-October, with the heaviest rain in the afternoon and evening. The best plan is to go out in the morning, stay indoors in the afternoon and pick places near the rail lines, such as BACC at Siam, the TCDC library and board-game cafés.

By the Kring team · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

What the rainy season is like

Thailand's Meteorological Department says the rainy season begins around the end of the second week of May and runs until about mid-October, sometimes with a drier lull from mid-June to mid-July. In the city, rain usually arrives as heavy afternoon or evening storms rather than all-day drizzle. Plan around that rhythm and the season is still a great time to be out.

Simple rainy-season rules

  • Go early: mornings are often sunny, so do outdoor things before noon.
  • Afternoons indoors: choose places where you can stay a while.
  • Stay near the rail lines: in a downpour, traffic jams and rides are scarce. The BTS and MRT save the day.
  • Check the rain radar before moving on; a weather app helps a lot.

Indoor places to go with friends

  • Bangkok Art and Culture Centre at Siam: linked to BTS National Stadium so you stay dry. Free exhibitions, Tuesday to Sunday 10:00–20:00.
  • TCDC in the Grand Postal Building: a whole-floor design library and exhibitions. Closed Mondays; day passes and annual memberships available.
  • Board-game cafés such as The Stronghold in Siam Square Soi 9 or Quest on the 7th floor of MBK, about 60 baht per person per hour (The Thaiger, October 2025). See board-game cafés in Bangkok.
  • Malls linked to stations around Siam, Asok and Phrom Phong, connected by covered walkways, with cinemas, bookshops and places to sit.
  • Co-working spaces, if you need to get work done while you wait it out.

A rainy-day plan with friends

  • 10:00 gather at BACC in Siam and see the exhibitions before it gets busy.
  • 13:00 lunch in a mall nearby.
  • 14:30 board games in Siam Square through the worst of the storm.
  • 18:00 the rain eases; head home by BTS.

Getting around when it pours

  • Do not book a car the moment rain starts; wait for it to ease and both price and wait time improve.
  • Some roads flood quickly. Avoid wading through water; you cannot see what is underneath.
  • Carry a small folding umbrella and a waterproof pouch for your phone.
  • Wear sandals or shoes that can get wet if you will walk a lot.

The upside of the rains

It is cooler than the hot season, the parks are lush and green, and after a storm the air is clear enough for long views. Sunsets after rain are often the best of the year. If it stops before evening, head to Chao Phraya Sky Park or the Benjakitti skywalk. See sunset spots in Bangkok.

Rainy-season bag checklist

  • A small folding umbrella or a light rain jacket.
  • A waterproof pouch for your phone and cards.
  • A power bank, in case you are waiting out a long storm.
  • A light layer: malls and trains are heavily air-conditioned.

Making friends in the rainy season

People stay in more, but indoor activities are at their busiest: workshops, board games and exhibitions. It is a good time to find people who like the same things you do.

FAQ

When is the rainy season in Bangkok?

The Thai Meteorological Department says it starts around mid-May and ends around mid-October.

What time of day does it rain in Bangkok?

Usually as heavy storms in the afternoon or evening. Mornings are often sunny.

What can I do in Bangkok when it rains?

Visit the Bangkok Art and Culture Centre, the TCDC library, board-game cafés and malls linked to rail stations.

How should I get around in heavy rain?

Use the BTS and MRT, wait for the rain to ease before booking a car, and avoid wading through floodwater.
