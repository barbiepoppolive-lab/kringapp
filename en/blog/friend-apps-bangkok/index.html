Best Apps to Make Friends in Bangkok: 5 Compared Honestly

For interest-based groups, start with LINE OpenChat and Bumble BFF; for structured first hangouts with strangers, Timeleft fits better. Kring, which is our own app, suits people who want to make friends around real Bangkok places, Thai and foreign alike.

By the Kring team · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

Disclosure first

This article is written by the Kring team, and Kring is on the list. We have tried to be straight about the strengths and limits of every option, ours included. Everything was checked in November 2026; features and prices change, so confirm in each app before you commit.

LINE OpenChat: hobby groups Thais already use

Almost everyone in Thailand has LINE. OpenChat is its searchable group-chat feature, with rooms for running crews, badminton, climbing, language practice and much more. Most rooms are in Thai, so this suits Thais and foreigners comfortable with some Thai.

  • Best for: people who know what they enjoy and want a group that actually does things together.
  • Limits: rooms are run by volunteer admins, so quality varies a lot. Some are silent, some carry a lot of adverts. Join a few and keep the ones where plans really happen.

Bumble BFF: swiping for friends, now with groups

Bumble's friend mode became a standalone app, Bumble BFF, which was revamped in September 2025 with a Groups tab: create or join groups, chat in group rooms and plan hangouts with an in-app calendar (per TechCrunch).

  • Best for: people who want friends of a similar age and are used to swipe-style apps.
  • Limits: the September 2025 coverage does not confirm which features are live in every country, so check what is available in Thailand.

Timeleft: dinner with four to six strangers

Timeleft seats strangers together at one table, in groups of four to six. Bangkok is on its list of cities; you choose a day in the app, and you must be 18+.

  • Best for: shy people who would rather not start conversations cold, because the format does it for you.
  • Limits: it is a paid service, and the group changes every time, so if you click with someone you need to swap contacts yourselves.

InterNations: the expat network

InterNations is a community for people who have moved abroad, with social events and interest groups in Bangkok.

  • Best for: newcomers from overseas, and Thais who want international friends.
  • Limits: it leans towards networking-style evenings, often in restaurants or hotels. There are free and paid memberships, but a night out usually costs more than a park run.

Strava and run clubs

If you run or cycle, Strava clubs and run-club Instagram accounts are a shortcut. Many groups are free with no sign-up; you just turn up on time. See running in Bangkok.

Kring: friends through real places (our app)

Kring is built for Bangkok. You make a profile card, post short Reels from spots you love and see who else loves them. There is a weekly board with real prizes for the best Reels, and an "Ask a Thai" space for questions about the city. Chats translate between Thai and English automatically.

  • Best for: Thais who want new friends and to show their side of Bangkok, and travellers who want to talk to people who actually live here.
  • Limits: Kring opens on 1 December 2026, so early on it has fewer members than the big apps. It is Bangkok-only for now, and it is for friends, not romance.

Red flags in any friend app

  • Talk of investing, crypto "trading", selling something or borrowing money within days.
  • Refusing to hang out in public, or insisting on picking you up from home.
  • Pushing to move the chat to another app straight away for no reason.
  • Model-perfect photos with no ordinary everyday pictures at all.

If you see these, stop replying and report the account. A good app makes reporting and blocking easy.

How to choose

  • Want an activity crew: LINE OpenChat or a Strava running club.
  • Want friends your own age in small groups: Bumble BFF.
  • Want someone to set the table for you: Timeleft.
  • Want friends around places you love in Bangkok, Thai and foreign: Kring.

You do not need just one. Two apps plus one real weekly activity works far better than five apps you never open.

FAQ

How is a friend app different from a romance app?

Friend apps are designed around friendship, usually groups and activities, not romantic matching. If you want real friends, an app built for friends is the better tool.

Which friend apps are free in Bangkok?

LINE OpenChat and Kring are free. Bumble BFF is free to download with optional paid features. Timeleft is a paid service; check current prices in the app.

Are friend apps safe to use?

Yes, if you keep first hangouts in public places, tell someone where you are going and travel on your own. Never share your address or money details with someone you have just met online.
