Talat Noi and Charoen Krung: Old Riverside Bangkok With Friends

Talat Noi and Charoen Krung form one of the best old riverside areas to explore with friends: street art, a Chinese mansion more than 200 years old and creative spaces such as TCDC. Take the MRT to Hua Lamphong or a river boat to Si Phraya Pier and walk from there.

By the Kring team · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

✓ Facts on this page last checked 17 Nov 2026 · we re-check this page every 3 months

Where it is

Talat Noi is in Samphanthawong district on the east bank of the Chao Phraya, an old community going back to the early Rattanakosin period, right next to Yaowarat. Charoen Krung Road runs south from here towards Bang Rak, past old buildings, galleries and creative spaces. See our Talat Noi area page.

Getting there

  • MRT Hua Lamphong: take Exit 3 and walk about 900 m to Soi Charoen Krung 22.
  • Chao Phraya Express Boat: get off at Si Phraya Pier and walk past the Holy Rosary Church into Talat Noi.

Talat Noi highlights

  • The street-art walls in Soi Chao Sua Son: a phoenix over the river, a figure-eight dragon, a girl with a fan and a golden frog that comes alive in AR through the Artivive app. According to the Bangkok Post, the murals followed residents' complaints about visitors leaning on their walls and making noise in the lane.
  • So Heng Tai: a roughly 230-year-old Chinese mansion at the end of Soi Chao Sua Son, one of the community's landmarks.
  • Small riverside lanes: heritage workshops, riverside cafés in restored warehouses and wooden houses where people still live.

This is a living neighbourhood. Walk quietly, do not lean on walls, do not photograph inside people's homes without asking and keep your voice down in the lanes.

Charoen Krung: the creative side

  • TCDC in the Grand Postal Building: a whole-floor design library with more than 18,000 art and design books, a silent reading area and a rooftop garden by landscape studio Shma. Closed Mondays. Time Out lists day passes and annual memberships; check current prices before entering.
  • Warehouse 30 on Charoen Krung Soi 30: old warehouses turned into a creative complex with shops, a bookshop, cafés and space for workshops and exhibitions. Tenants change over time.
  • Small galleries all along the road, many free to enter.

A half-day route

  • 14:00 MRT Hua Lamphong, walk into Talat Noi for the street art.
  • 15:00 So Heng Tai and the riverside lanes.
  • 16:00 walk or take a short ride to Warehouse 30 while its shops are still open.
  • 17:15 on to TCDC and up to the rooftop, then a boat from a nearby pier to see the river at dusk.

On to Yaowarat or across the river

Talat Noi borders Yaowarat, a few minutes' walk away, where the neon comes on beautifully after dark. For a change of scene, take a cross-river ferry to the Thonburi side and sit by the water looking back at this bank at sunset, a view most visitors never see.

Why it works for a group of new friends

There is something to look at every few steps, so conversation comes naturally, and it is slow and quiet with no loud music. It is a great plan for a few new friends from an app or your run club, and almost every corner makes a good photo.

After dark in the old lanes

Some of Talat Noi's narrow lanes are dark and quiet after nightfall. Walk as a group, use your phone torch on uneven ground, and come out onto well-lit Charoen Krung Road before booking a ride home.

If it rains

Talat Noi's lanes are narrow and slippery in the rain. Shelter in TCDC, a big indoor building with the library and exhibitions, or under the roofs of Warehouse 30. When it stops, the old lanes look even better in photos.

Tips

  • Come in the late afternoon for softer light and less heat.
  • Wear comfortable shoes; lane surfaces are uneven.
  • Many Charoen Krung spaces close on Mondays, so another day shows you more.
  • To stay for sunset, see sunset spots in Bangkok.

FAQ

How do I get to Talat Noi?

Take the MRT to Hua Lamphong, Exit 3, and walk about 900 m to Soi Charoen Krung 22, or take the Chao Phraya Express Boat to Si Phraya Pier.

Where is So Heng Tai?

At the end of Soi Chao Sua Son in Talat Noi. It is a Chinese mansion around 230 years old.

When is TCDC open?

TCDC in the Grand Postal Building is closed on Mondays and open Tuesday to Sunday. Check current hours and prices before you go.

How long do I need for Talat Noi?

Two to three hours for Talat Noi itself; allow half a day if you add Charoen Krung.
