Running in Bangkok 2027: The Best Parks and Run Clubs to Join

The best places to run in Bangkok are Lumphini Park, Benjakitti Forest Park and Suan Rot Fai. For running friends, several clubs are free with no sign-up, such as SarDine Run Club on Saturdays and URT at Benjakitti most mornings.

By the Kring team · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

Why running is a great way to make friends

Running together does not need constant conversation, costs nothing and puts you with the same people every week. The stretch before and the coffee after are when people really get to know each other, which is why Bangkok's run-club scene has grown so fast in recent years.

The parks

  • Lumphini Park: open 04:30–21:00, with a loop of about 2.5 km, close to BTS Sala Daeng and MRT Si Lom and Lumphini. Busy morning and evening. Bikes are officially allowed only 10:00–15:00.
  • Benjakitti Forest Park: a 72-hectare forest park fully opened in 2022, near MRT Queen Sirikit National Convention Centre. The elevated 1.3 km "Green Bridge" links it to Lumphini, so you can string together a long run.
  • Suan Rot Fai (Wachirabenchathat Park) near Chatuchak: shady, wide paths and bike rental, good for longer efforts.
  • Chatuchak Park and Queen Sirikit Park sit right next door, so you can extend a Suan Rot Fai run.

Run clubs to try

Per Time Out (February 2026). Check each club's Instagram before going, because schedules change.

  • SarDine Run Club: Saturday runs, free with no sign-up, rotating between Suan Rot Fai, Lumphini and Benjakitti, plus pop-up city runs.
  • Ugly Running Training Club (URT): most mornings around 6:30am at Gate 3 of Benjakitti Park, by the stretching zone. No membership tiers.
  • Sabai Run Club: Sunday mornings, plus Friday-night runs with more than a thousand people.
  • Meep Meep Run Club: twice-monthly morning sessions with theCOMMONS (check the club's Instagram for which branch and route), starting around 7am; arrive 15 minutes early for the briefing.
  • Cruise Control Run Club: follow its Instagram for schedules.

Running in Bangkok weather

  • Run before 7am or after 6pm and skip the midday sun.
  • Hydrate beforehand and carry water or cash for an electrolyte drink.
  • In the dry season, check PM2.5 air quality; on bad days move your workout indoors.
  • In the rainy season, park paths get slippery, so ease off on corners.

Your first run-club session

  • Arrive a little early and tell the organiser it is your first time.
  • Ask about a slower pace group; most clubs have one.
  • Stay for food or coffee afterwards. That is where the friendships happen.
  • Follow the club on Strava or Instagram so you see next week's plan.

What to bring

  • A small water bottle, or cash for drinks.
  • A charged phone, to swap contacts and book a ride home.
  • A towel and a spare shirt if you will stay for breakfast.
  • Sunscreen, even for a morning run.

You do not need expensive shoes or a GPS watch for your first session; comfortable trainers are enough.

Not a runner yet?

Start with brisk walks around Lumphini Park or a bike ride at Suan Rot Fai. Some clubs have walking or very easy pace groups, and plenty of members started from zero too. Showing up every week matters far more than speed.

After the run

Run Suan Rot Fai and walk on to Chatuchak Weekend Market on a Saturday or Sunday, or join a session at theCOMMONS and stay for coffee around Thonglor.

FAQ

Where is the best place to run in Bangkok?

Lumphini Park (a loop of about 2.5 km), Benjakitti Forest Park, linked to Lumphini by the 1.3 km Green Bridge, and shady, spacious Suan Rot Fai.

Are Bangkok run clubs free?

Many are free with no sign-up, such as SarDine Run Club per Time Out (February 2026), though special events may need registration.

What time does Lumphini Park open?

Daily from 04:30 to 21:00.

When is the best time to run in Bangkok?

Before 7am or after 6pm, when it is cooler and most clubs run.
