Things to Do Alone in Bangkok: 12 Ideas That Feel Easy

The best things to do alone in Bangkok are places where everyone comes to look, listen or wander quietly: art centres, libraries, parks and the Chao Phraya river boats. Here are 12 we like, with the best time for each.

By the Kring team · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

✓ Hours, prices and transport on this page last checked 17 Nov 2026 · we re-check this page every month

Alone does not mean lonely

Plenty of people in Bangkok go out on their own, and at the places below it is completely normal. Several of them also make it easy to start talking to someone without trying. Visiting and hoping to find people to hang out with? See solo travel in Bangkok.

Art and ideas

1. Bangkok Art and Culture Centre (BACC) at Siam: free exhibitions, open Tuesday to Sunday 10:00–20:00, closed Mondays, with a direct link from BTS National Stadium. The lower floors have bookshops and small cafés. 2. TCDC in the Grand Postal Building, Bang Rak: a design library filling a whole floor, a silent reading area and a rooftop garden. Closed Mondays. Time Out lists day passes and annual memberships; check current prices at the desk. 3. Independent bookshops and small galleries in Ari and along Charoen Krung Road: browse as long as you like.

Walks and parks

4. Lumphini Park, open 04:30–21:00, with a roughly 2.5 km loop, pedal boats on the lake and very relaxed monitor lizards. 5. The Benjakitti skywalk, a 1.3 km elevated green bridge from Lumphini Park to Benjakitti Forest Park, for walking or cycling above the city. 6. Chao Phraya Sky Park on the Phra Pok Klao Bridge, open 05:00–20:00 and free; best just before sunset. 7. Talat Noi: street art, the So Heng Tai mansion and old riverside lanes. Two or three hours is about right.

On the river

8. The Chao Phraya Express Boat: ride it without rushing to get off. Watching both banks go by is one of the cheapest and best ways to see the city. 9. The Thonburi riverside: breezier and quieter than the old-town side in the evening, with riverside decks for watching the boats.

Do your own thing

10. A shophouse café: many in Ari and Talat Noi have long tables or single seats for an afternoon of reading, journaling or work. 11. A co-working day pass: good seats, strong air-con and familiar faces to say hello to. See Bangkok for digital nomads for current day-pass prices. 12. A board-game café: going solo is fine, and staff often teach games or find you a table. See board-game cafés in Bangkok.

A sample solo day

  • 09:00 walk Lumphini Park, then take the skywalk to Benjakitti.
  • 12:00 escape the heat at BACC in Siam: exhibitions, then a bookshop.
  • 15:30 take the MRT to Hua Lamphong and walk into Talat Noi.
  • 17:30 take a short ride (10–15 minutes) to Chao Phraya Sky Park for sunset.

From a solo day to new friends

A solo day does not have to end solo. If someone is photographing the same view, offer to take their picture or ask which angle looks better. In a gallery or bookshop, asking what someone thinks of the piece in front of you is a natural opener.

Make a solo day better

  • Go early or late, and skip noon to 3pm, the hottest part of the day.
  • Use the BTS, MRT and river boats; they beat sitting in traffic.
  • Stick to one area per outing. Walking it properly beats racing across town.
  • If you would like company next time, post where you are going on a friend app; someone who loves the same spot may be nearby.
  • Out alone after dark? Share your location with someone close, and head home by train or a booked ride.

FAQ

What can I do alone in Bangkok without feeling lonely?

The Bangkok Art and Culture Centre, the TCDC library, Lumphini Park, Chao Phraya Sky Park and board-game cafés all have lots of solo visitors and plenty to do.

Is the Bangkok Art and Culture Centre free?

Exhibitions are free except for some special events. It opens Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–20:00, and is closed on Mondays.

What time does Lumphini Park open?

Daily from 04:30 to 21:00. The loop path is about 2.5 km.

When is the best time of day to explore alone?

Early morning or after 5pm, when it is cooler and Bangkok people come out to walk, run and sit in the parks.
