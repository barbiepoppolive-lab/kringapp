Solo Travel in Bangkok 2027: Where to Find People

The easiest places to make friends when travelling Bangkok solo are activities where others also come alone: cooking or Muay Thai classes, run clubs, board-game cafés and guesthouse socials. In the evenings, go where people hang out in groups, like night markets and the riverside parks.

By the Kring team · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

The one rule

Go where other people also turn up alone, and where there is something to do together. That removes the hardest part, the opening line, because the activity starts the conversation for you. (Live here and just want a calm day out on your own? That is a different plan: see things to do alone in Bangkok.)

Mornings: run clubs and parks

Bangkok is hot, so the city exercises at dawn and after dark. Several run clubs welcome visitors with no sign-up. SarDine Run Club runs on Saturdays, rotating between Suan Rot Fai, Lumphini Park and Benjakitti Park, and Ugly Running Training Club (URT) runs most mornings around 6:30am at Gate 3 of Benjakitti Park (per Time Out, February 2026). Post-run breakfast is when people actually talk.

Daytime: classes and culture

Thai cooking classes and Muay Thai classes are the two classics for solo travellers, because everyone starts from zero together and chats naturally between steps. Pick small groups taught in English and book a day or two ahead.

For art, the Bangkok Art and Culture Centre at Siam has free exhibitions and is open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–20:00, linked directly to BTS National Stadium. More ideas in free things to do in Bangkok.

Afternoons: board-game cafés

At a board-game café, playing with strangers is normal, and staff will often teach you the rules. Quest Board Game Café on the 7th floor of MBK Center charges about 60 baht per person per hour (per The Thaiger, October 2025). See our list of board-game cafés in Bangkok.

Evenings: riverside and night markets

  • Chao Phraya Sky Park sits on the Phra Pok Klao Bridge, open daily 05:00–20:00 and free. Walk across from the old town to the Thonburi side for sunset.
  • Jodd Fairs Ratchada opens daily from about 17:00 to 01:00, a few minutes' walk from MRT Thailand Cultural Centre Exit 4. (The old Rama 9 site closed in January 2025.) Shared tables make it easy to chat with the people next to you.
  • Talat Noi is best in the late afternoon, for street art and old riverside houses.

More in night markets to go to with friends.

Where to stay if you want company

If making friends on the way is the goal, where you sleep matters more than you think. Guesthouses and backpacker stays with a proper common room and a weekly events board make it much easier, so read what activities a place actually runs before you book, and stay near a BTS or MRT station so getting around is simple.

A simple three-day solo plan

  • Day 1: Lumphini Park in the morning, BACC at Siam in the afternoon, Jodd Fairs Ratchada in the evening.
  • Day 2: a cooking or Muay Thai class in the morning, Talat Noi in the afternoon, Chao Phraya Sky Park at sunset.
  • Day 3: a run club in the morning (if it is a Saturday), a board-game café in the afternoon, an evening ride on the Chao Phraya Express Boat.

Staying safe on your own

  • Hang out with new people in public places, and get around by BTS, MRT or a ride-hailing app you booked yourself.
  • If a friendly stranger says the place you are heading to is closed today, or offers to take you to a shop "run by a friend", decline politely and check for yourself. It is a well-known scam pattern.
  • Leave your passport in the hotel safe and carry a copy.
  • Share your location with someone at home when you go somewhere new at night.

Small things that help

Wearing a run-club shirt, or learning a few Thai phrases, starts conversations on its own. See Thai phrases and etiquette for making Thai friends. And if you want to know where Bangkok people are actually going this week, ask someone who lives here; it beats any list.

FAQ

Is Bangkok safe for solo travellers?

Generally yes, with normal city awareness. Use the BTS, MRT or a booked ride-hailing car, keep first hangouts in public places, and be wary of strangers who say your destination is closed.

Where is the easiest place to make friends as a solo traveller in Bangkok?

Cooking or Muay Thai classes, run clubs, board-game cafés and guesthouse social events, because most people there came alone and there is an activity to share.

What are Chao Phraya Sky Park's opening hours?

The latest listing we found says it is open daily from 05:00 to 20:00, and entry is free.
