Things to Do in Ari, Bangkok

The best things to do in Ari are slow ones: walk the leafy sois behind BTS Ari, browse Gump's Ari and small galleries, sit in a café you like and come back until you are a regular. Most people here actually live here, which makes Ari one of the easiest Bangkok areas to hang out with friends.

By the Kring team · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

Where Ari is

Ari sits on Phahonyothin Road at BTS Ari on the Sukhumvit line, two stops from Chatuchak Weekend Market. The sois behind the station are shaded by big trees and lined with old houses turned into shops, and the low-rise buildings make it feel like a small town inside a big city. For an overview, see our Ari area page.

Places worth a stop

From Time Out (May 2026). Shops open, close and move, so check each place's page before going.

  • Gump's Ari: a cluster of small shops, boutiques and rotating pop-ups, good for a whole afternoon.
  • Somewhere: an exhibition and workshop space on Soi Pradipat 17, a short ride towards Saphan Khwai, ideal for making friends with creative people.
  • KICHgallery: contemporary art.
  • La Villa: near the station, with restaurants, a supermarket and open-air seating. An easy landmark for a first hangout.
  • Cafés with character, such as flower-filled Thongyoy.
  • Cafe Amazon Experience: a branch designed as a community space, good for long sits and chats.

Why Ari is good for making friends

In a neighbourhood where people actually live, you see the same faces again. The people in a café on a Saturday morning are often locals, and after two or three visits you start saying hello, unlike visitor-heavy areas where the crowd changes daily. Ari's workshop spaces also mean there is always something new to join.

Half a day in Ari

  • 10:00 BTS Ari, then into the sois for a morning coffee.
  • 11:30 browse small shops and pop-ups at Gump's Ari.
  • 13:30 a gallery, or check what is on at Somewhere.
  • 15:30 rest at La Villa, or ride two stops to Chatuchak at weekends.

Working in Ari

Plenty of people work from Ari's cafés. If you stay all day, order something every so often and avoid the lunch rush. For a proper desk, co-working day passes in Bangkok start at around 250 baht (September 2026).

Making friends in Ari

  • Be a regular at one café: same day, same time.
  • Follow the local workshop spaces and sign up for classes that run over several weeks.
  • Join a Saturday run club, then come back to Ari for coffee; some clubs run at Suan Rot Fai, not far away.
  • Suggest a walk around Ari to a few new friends. The sois are quiet enough to actually talk.

Best times to go

  • Weekday mornings: locals grab coffee before work; the calmest atmosphere.
  • Weekend late mornings: busy, with queues at famous cafés, but the most pop-ups and events.
  • Weekday evenings: walk the sois after work as shop lights come on and the air cools.

A first visit with new friends

Pick an easy landmark, such as BTS Ari or La Villa, then walk into the sois together. Do not over-plan: wandering until something catches your eye is the point of Ari. If the group gets on, suggest coming back on a day with a workshop or pop-up, so you have a reason to hang out again.

If it rains

Rain is not a big problem in Ari: most places are close together with awnings. Duck into a gallery or a café where you can sit for a while, wait for it to ease, then carry on, or ride the BTS a few stops to BACC at Siam.

Small tips

  • Many Ari places close on Mondays or Tuesdays; check before you set off.
  • Popular cafés have queues at weekends; arrive before 10am for a good seat.
  • Walk rather than drive; the sois are narrow and traffic builds in the evening.

FAQ

Which station is Ari?

BTS Ari on the Sukhumvit line. The sois are right behind the station and easy to walk.

What is there to do in Ari besides cafés?

Galleries, exhibition and workshop spaces, a cluster of small shops and pop-ups, and Chatuchak Weekend Market is two stops away.

Is Ari good for making friends?

Yes. People live there, so you see the same faces often, and workshop spaces run repeat classes.

When are Ari shops closed?

It varies; many close on Mondays or Tuesdays. Check each place's page before you go.
