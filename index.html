FAQ

Good questions

How does payment work?

You pay in the app when your host accepts. The money is held safely by a licensed payment partner until you meet. When your phones touch — กริ๊ง! — it is released to your host. Paid money never expires.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel 24 hours or more before and you get a full refund. Sick or bad weather? Full refund. If your host can’t come, you get a full refund too.

What if my phone dies?

Use the 6-digit backup code instead. Your host enters it and the meeting is confirmed, just like touching phones.

We’re more than 2 people. Can we come?

Each activity booking is for 1 or 2 people. For bigger groups, choose a licensed group tour in the Deals tab.

Do I need to speak Thai?

No. Chat translates automatically between English, Thai, Chinese, Korean, Hindi, Russian and Malay.

Can I tip my host?

If you’d like to, after the activity. Tips go to your host.
