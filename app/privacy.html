Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: October 23, 2026 · Last Updated: October 23, 2026 · Kring (กริ๊ง!) · Bangkok & Pattaya, Thailand

1. Overview & Public-Venue Cultural Platform Commitment

Kring (กริ๊ง!) operates a 20+ cultural and lifestyle marketplace connecting travellers and residents with ID-verified local insiders for public-venue experiences (street food walks, temple mornings, rooftop cafés, licensed spas, and city navigation) alongside Kring Price partner passes in Bangkok and Pattaya, Thailand.

We comply strictly with the Thailand Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA) and Google Play User Data & Data Safety Policies.

2. Information We Collect & Purpose

  • Account & Identity Information (20+ Age Gate): Display name, phone number or email address, age confirmation (strictly 20+ only), and preferred language (EN/TH).
  • Ephemeral 1-Face Verification (Anti-Catfish Safety): For verified local hosts, a live camera check generates a non-reversible mathematical verification vector to confirm profile authenticity. Raw biometric verification photos are never sold, never shared with third parties, and are permanently purged upon account deletion.
  • Public-Venue Bump Check-In (Location & Proximity): When you check in with your Local at a public meeting point using the Two-Phone กริ๊ง! Bump or rotating QR code, approximate/precise location is verified solely at the moment of check-in to release the held booking escrow safely. Background location tracking is never used when the app is closed.
  • In-App Chat & Automated Contact Masking: Chat messages and offer cards are processed to facilitate scheduling and automatic EN/TH translation. Pre-booking chats automatically mask external phone numbers, LINE IDs, and QR codes to protect both parties from off-platform fraud.
  • Double-Entry Transaction Ledger: Top-ups, held booking escrows, check-in releases, refunds, and PromptPay/partner payouts are recorded with cryptographic idempotency keys for financial integrity.

3. Strict Host Independence & Agency Privacy Wall

  • All Verified Locals are independent adults (20+) who control their own availability, pricing, and public venue preferences.
  • Bookings (who, where, when, price, and chat history) are visible only to the Guest and the Local.
  • Partner talent agencies receive aggregated performance totals only and never have access to private chats, guest identities, or live meeting locations.

4. Data Sharing, Encryption & Retention

  • Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All API traffic is encrypted via TLS 1.3; database tables enforce strict PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell user data or serve third-party behavioral ad networks.
  • Account & Biometric Deletion: You can delete your account and purge all personal/biometric data at any time inside the app (My Profile → Settings → Delete Account) or via our Web Account Deletion Portal.
