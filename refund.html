Refund & cancellation policy

Online you pay only the booking fee. You pay the activity price to your host at the meeting point, after check-in. If the activity doesn't happen, you never pay for it.

Effective 25 October 2026

1. What you get back

  • You cancel 24 hours or more before the start: full refund of the booking fee.
  • Your host cancels, or doesn't check in within 15 minutes of the start: full refund of the booking fee, automatically.
  • Kring cancels (for example for safety or weather): full refund of the booking fee.
  • You cancel less than 24 hours before the start: the booking fee is not refunded, but you can move the booking once, free, within 7 days. For documented emergencies (illness, cancelled flight) you can ask us to review.
  • Unused partner digital vouchers: full refund within 14 days of purchase, if the code has not been scanned.

2. The activity price paid at the meeting point

You pay the activity price straight to your host after check-in, so Kring never holds it. If the activity isn't as described, report it within 2 hours after the planned end. We review it within 3 working days, ask the host to refund you, refund your booking fee where fair, and remove hosts who break the rules. For tours by licensed tour companies, the tour price paid at their counter follows that company's refund policy.

3. How long refunds take

We start the refund within 24 hours of approval. It reaches your original payment method (card, UPI or wallet) within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

4. How to ask for a refund

Cancel from the booking in the app, or email your booking number to ops@kringapp.com. We reply within 24 hours.
