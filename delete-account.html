Delete your Kring account and data

You can delete your Kring (กริ๊ง!) account and personal data inside the app, or by email if you no longer have the app.

Option A: in the Kring app

  1. Open the Kring app and tap Me (ฉัน) in the bottom bar.
  2. Tap Settings (ตั้งค่า).
  3. Tap Delete account (ลบบัญชี) and confirm. Your account is deleted for good 7 days after you confirm. Sign in again within those 7 days if you change your mind.

Option B: by email (no app needed)

Email ops@kringapp.com from the email address on your account (or include the phone number you signed up with) and write "Delete my Kring account". We reply within 48 hours to confirm it is you, then delete the account. You can also ask us to delete only your ID-check data and photos and keep the account.

Email a deletion request →

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your name, email and phone, profile photos, preferences (such as language, gender filter and diet), emergency contact, chats, saved activities, and any host ID-check data and selfie.
  • Kept, as the law requires: records of payments you made to Kring (when and how much) for tax and fraud checks, for up to 5 years, without your profile.
  • Bookings: any upcoming booking is cancelled and its Kring ticket refunded in full before the account is closed.
